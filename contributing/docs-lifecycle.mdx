This policy defines how documentation pages move through lifecycle states.

## Lifecycle states

- **Active**: Current and recommended documentation.
- **Legacy**: Kept for compatibility context; not recommended for new builds.
- **Deprecated**: Scheduled for retirement with a documented replacement path.
- **Hidden**: Not shown in primary navigation; must have explicit ownership and reason.

## Required metadata and behavior

- Deprecated and hidden pages should include migration guidance to an active page.
- Hidden pages should use `hidden: true` and `noindex: true` when applicable.
- Deprecated pages should be listed in [Deprecation Index](/contributing/deprecations).

## Hidden-page governance

For each hidden page, maintain:

1. Business reason for hidden status
2. Owning team/person
3. Planned review date
4. Removal or reactivation decision

## Review cadence

- Review hidden and deprecated pages on a regular cadence (recommended monthly or per release cycle).
- Remove pages with no support need and no meaningful inbound traffic.
